Questions

How fast will I see results?

That depends on your starting point, your consistency and your sleep, and anyone who gives you a number without knowing you is guessing. What I can promise is a plan you can actually keep to, and honest feedback every month.

I'm a complete beginner. Is this for me?

Yes. Tell me that in the questionnaire and your first weeks get built to teach you the movements before anything gets heavy.

I don't have a gym membership.

Fine. Tell me what you do have — dumbbells, bands, a pull-up bar, or nothing at all — and the plan is written around that.

I'm vegetarian, vegan, coeliac, or allergic to something.

Say so in the questionnaire and it won't appear in your plan. If you're managing a medical condition, have a history of disordered eating, or you're pregnant, please talk to your doctor or a registered dietitian first — that's outside what I can coach.

Can I message you if something isn't working?

On the monthly plan, yes, any time through the week. On the one-off plan you get one round of changes after you receive it.
